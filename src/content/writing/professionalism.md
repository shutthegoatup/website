## Power dynamics

I like to punch up. Sometimes across. Rarely down.

A couple of weeks back I heard a story that in a leadership call I created,
with leaders I hired, they were discussing diminishing and trying to remove a
member of the team because they disagreed with them.

This is extremely disheartening. I really don't want to believe it. But I know
the personalities involved, and I know it's likely true. Worse still, I know it
probably took a good deal of courage to disagree with this particular person,
and that the person disagreeing was probably on the right side of the
discussion based on merit.

But they're gonna get fucked anyway, because this is what we deem an
engineering culture in 2024.
